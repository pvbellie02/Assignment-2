About the Guide

The Campus Event Guide is maintained by the Office of Student Engagement to make it easier for students to find things to do outside of class. Every event listed here is either hosted or co-sponsored by a recognized campus organization.

Whether you are looking to build your resume, unwind after a long week, or just try something new, there is usually something happening within walking distance of your dorm.

  • One central calendar instead of dozens of separate club pages
  • Event details written by students, for students
  • Direct links to registration and accessibility information
