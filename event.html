Culture

International Food Festival

Sunday, October 19, 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM · Campus Green

The International Food Festival brings together more than a dozen student cultural organizations for an afternoon of food, music, and performance on the Campus Green. It is one of the most attended events of the fall semester and welcomes students, staff, and the surrounding community.

About This Event

Each participating organization runs its own food station, offering dishes rooted in a specific country or region. Proceeds from ticket sales go directly back to the student organizations to fund their programming for the rest of the year. A stage at the center of the Green hosts short performances throughout the afternoon, including dance groups and a cappella sets.

What to Expect

  • Twelve to fifteen food stations, each selling small tasting portions for tickets
  • A performance stage with sets roughly every 30 minutes
  • Seating areas on the Green, plus overflow seating near the library steps
  • A lost-and-found and first aid tent near the main entrance

Schedule

  1. 11:00 AM - Gates open, food stations begin serving
  2. 11:30 AM - Opening performance from the Ashbrook Dance Collective
  3. 12:30 PM - Cooking demo hosted by the Culinary Club
  4. 2:00 PM - Closing performance and organization shout-outs
  5. 3:00 PM - Event ends, stations close

Accessibility & Participation

The Campus Green is fully paved and wheelchair accessible from both the library and Student Union entrances. Reserved seating near the stage is available on request at the information tent. Several stations offer vegetarian and vegan options, and ingredient lists are posted at each table for common allergens.
